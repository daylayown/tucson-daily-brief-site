From the Editor

Talk to us

Tucson Daily Brief has always been a two-way street — and there are now more ways than ever to follow along and talk back.

Every feed does its own thing. We don’t paste the same post everywhere: X gets the story threads, Instagram gets the fun, Bluesky gets the conversation. Follow wherever you already spend your time — and say hello when you do.

Email

The simplest way to reach us hasn’t changed: write to editor@tucsondailybrief.com. TDB founder and editor Nicholas reads every email — tips, corrections, leads, and friendly hellos are all welcome.

Comment on any story

Every TDB story now has comments, powered by Bluesky. Look for the comments section at the bottom of an article: it links to that story’s Bluesky post, and replies there appear right on the page.

Bluesky

We’re @tucsondailybrief.com — yes, the handle is our web address. New to Bluesky or want a better Tucson feed? Our Tucson News & Community starter pack follows 34 local voices in one tap.

X

New this week: we’re on X at @tusdailybrief, where the biggest Tucson stories get told as proper threads — the whole story, right in your feed.

Instagram & Threads

On Instagram we share the fun side — behind-the-scenes stories and reels from around the Old Pueblo. On Threads you’ll find quicker takes as stories publish.

Facebook

The TDB Facebook page carries the day’s headlines — an easy follow if Facebook is your home base.

Listen or watch

The daily brief is also a morning podcast — on Apple Podcasts — and our YouTube channel has a daily short if you’d rather watch your news in under a minute.

Ask ChatTDB

Curious about something around town? ChatTDB answers questions about Tucson using everything TDB has published, with citations to the original stories.

And for the best of the week in one place, the free Sunday newsletter is below. However you find us — welcome in.

TDB Weekly

A warm Sunday-morning roundup of Tucson, in your inbox.

The week’s most important Tucson developments, what local government decided, and what’s coming next — plus The Tucson Mini, a 5×5 crossword built just for subscribers.

Free. Sunday mornings. Unsubscribe anytime.

By Nicholas De Leon, in Tucson.
