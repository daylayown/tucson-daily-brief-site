Daily Brief

August 7, 2026

Friday

🏛️ Government

City Council adopts strict data center zoning rules in 6-1 vote. The Tucson City Council voted 6-1 Wednesday to adopt new zoning regulations for large-scale data centers, requiring rezoning review, water and noise safeguards and a half-mile setback from homes. Arizona Luminaria reports the mayor and council also directed city staff to keep studying stricter rules or a potential outright ban after residents told the council the proposed regulations did not go far enough.

Arizona Daily Star, AZPM, Arizona Luminaria

Legal challenge filed against Proposition 144. A city councilman has filed a legal challenge to Proposition 144, arguing that the measure's five key provisions should be put to voters separately rather than as a single question, according to the Arizona Daily Star. The measure is slated for the statewide ballot that Pima County voters will decide.

Arizona Daily Star

Sun on Demand adds east side zone Aug. 16. The City of Tucson said in a release that its free, on-demand transit service will launch a new east side zone beginning Sunday, Aug. 16, covering the area from Tanque Verde Road to Golf Links Road and Wilmot Road to Houghton Road. The city said it is the largest Sun on Demand zone to date, roughly double the size of existing zones.

City of Tucson, Sun Tran

🚨 Public Safety

One killed, one hurt crossing East Speedway. One man was killed and another seriously injured Tuesday night when they were struck by a car while crossing East Speedway near North Craycroft Road, Tucson police said. The Arizona Daily Star reported the account from police; no further details on the driver were released.

Arizona Daily Star

Downtown mass shooting suspect due for arraignment. The man accused in last month's mass shooting in downtown Tucson is scheduled to appear in court Friday for his arraignment, according to KVOA. Separately, two people who pleaded guilty in the killing of Paul Clifford are set to be sentenced Friday, the station reported.

KVOA News 4, KVOA News 4

Storm damage sends cleanup crews to Tucson parks. Cleanup crews were deployed to multiple Tucson parks after this week's storms caused widespread damage across the city, KVOA reported. Additional storm chances continue through the weekend.

KVOA News 4

🎓 Schools

Arizona advocates warn on proposed Head Start deregulation. Arizona Head Start leaders say a federal deregulation plan could reshape services for about 17,000 children in the state, KOLD reported. The Arizona Mirror reported the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services announced Thursday that it intends to significantly reduce regulations governing the early childhood program.

KOLD News 13, Arizona Mirror

🏗️ Development & Business

Del Lago Golf Club closing in Vail. Del Lago Golf Club, the centerpiece of the master-planned Rancho del Lago community in Vail, is closing, according to a statement from its owners reported by KGUN 9. KOLD reported the closure comes amid rising costs and declining demand, leaving employees looking for work and raising concerns about the local economy.

KGUN 9, KOLD News 13

Project Uptown nears first-phase completion at old Foothills Mall site. Construction on the first phase of Project Uptown, the redevelopment of the former Foothills Mall on Tucson's northside, is nearly finished, KGUN 9 reported. Developers are targeting a January 2027 opening for a Tempo by Hilton hotel at the site.

KGUN 9

Salad and Go files for bankruptcy, closes locations. The Arizona-based drive-thru chain Salad and Go has filed for bankruptcy and announced the immediate closure of all of its locations, according to AZPM. The company operated stores in the Tucson area.

AZPM

Three Points residents push back on proposed solar farm. Some Three Points residents and farmers are opposing a proposed solar project that would cover more than 5,500 acres of Arizona State Land near Robles Junction, KGUN 9 reported. Opponents raised concerns at a community meeting about the scale of the project.

KGUN 9

🎉 Community & Events

Mobile Meals merges with Community Food Bank. Mobile Meals of Southern Arizona has merged with the Community Food Bank of Southern Arizona, a move officials said was driven by long-term operational and financial sustainability, according to KOLD. Officials described the combination as a way to keep home-delivered meal service running.

KOLD News 13

Mutual aid groups shift schedules as heat persists. Community mutual aid groups including ALMA and Community Care Tucson are adjusting distribution hours and expanding hydration networks to address gaps in the city's heat response, Tucson Spotlight reported in a Spanish-language article.

Tucson Spotlight

📢 What Your Officials Are Saying

Sen. Mark Kelly (D-Ariz.). Kelly said in a release that he and colleagues are demanding the Trump administration stop what they describe as illegal voter roll purges ahead of Election Day.

Sen. Mark Kelly

Sens. Mark Kelly and Ruben Gallego (D-Ariz.). Both senators announced in releases that they are pressing the FAA over what they call a flawed Phoenix flight path plan; KOLD reported the senators say the plan would expose an additional 86,000 residents to aircraft noise.

Sen. Mark Kelly, Sen. Ruben Gallego

Sen. Ruben Gallego (D-Ariz.). Gallego announced in a release that he and Sen. Mike Rounds have introduced a bill they say would spur affordable housing investment.

Sen. Ruben Gallego

Sen. Ruben Gallego (D-Ariz.). In a separate release, Gallego said he introduced bipartisan legislation intended to protect veterans' disability pay.

Sen. Ruben Gallego

In the two competitive races the brief tracks, no campaign material was posted in the past 48 hours: neither Katie Hobbs nor Andy Biggs posted in the governor's race, and neither JoAnna Mendoza nor Juan Ciscomani posted in the 6th Congressional District race.
