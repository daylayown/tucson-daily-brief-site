Daily Brief

July 27, 2026

Monday

🏛️ Government

Amphitheater Public Schools to put property sales on November ballot. Amphitheater Public Schools is seeking voter approval on the Nov. 3 ballot to sell, lease, or exchange land and buildings the district no longer needs, according to Inside Tucson Business.

Inside Tucson Business

Gov. Hobbs holds "100 Days Out" rally in Tucson. Governor Katie Hobbs held a campaign rally in Tucson on Sunday, 100 days before Election Day, to rally Democratic support for her 2026 reelection bid.

KOLD News 13, KVOA News 4

Tohono O'odham Nation asks federal court to halt border wall construction. The Tohono O'odham Nation has filed a federal court request to stop border wall construction across tribal lands, citing sovereignty concerns, damage to sacred sites, and environmental harm, according to the Arizona Daily Star.

Arizona Daily Star

Pima County offers help navigating new SNAP work requirements. Pima County has announced it is ready to assist residents affected by federal changes that expand work requirements for the Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program, according to Inside Tucson Business.

Inside Tucson Business

🚨 Public Safety

Driver hospitalized after crashing into tree on River Road. A single-vehicle crash on East River Road near Via Entrada on Sunday shut down westbound traffic for several hours. The Pima County Sheriff's Department said the driver was transported to the hospital with life-threatening injuries but has since been listed as stable.

KGUN 9, KVOA News 4

🏗️ Development & Business

Downtown shooting prompts Empire Pizza to call off expansion. Tucson pizzeria Empire Pizza and Pub has indicated via social media that it is likely abandoning plans to expand into the Hydra building downtown, a decision that appears connected to last week's shooting in the area, according to the Arizona Daily Star.

Arizona Daily Star

Vandalism does not deter plans for desert EV lounge. A Tucson desert EV lounge project is moving forward despite vandalism at its bunker site, according to the Arizona Daily Star.

Arizona Daily Star

UA Tech Park startup converts captured CO₂ into aviation fuel. Tucson-based Dimensional Energy, operating out of the UA Tech Park, has developed technology that converts captured carbon dioxide into renewable oil usable for aviation fuel, diesel, and other products, according to the Arizona Daily Star.

Arizona Daily Star

TMC Health Foundation receives $500,000 grant for virtual care expansion. The TMC Health Foundation has received a $500,000 grant from the James M. Cox Foundation to expand virtual health care access at TMC Rincon and extend services to underserved populations across Southern Arizona, according to Inside Tucson Business.

Inside Tucson Business

🎉 Community & Events

Barrio Anita neighborhood distributes free school supplies. The Barrio Anita neighborhood hosted a back-to-school supply giveaway over the weekend, providing free materials to local families ahead of the new school year, according to KGUN 9.

KGUN 9
