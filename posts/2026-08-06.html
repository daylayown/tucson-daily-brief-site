Daily Brief

August 6, 2026

Thursday

🏛️ Government

City Council adopts strict rules for large-scale data centers. The Tucson City Council voted 6-1 late Wednesday to adopt zoning rules for large-scale data centers, requiring rezoning review and setting standards for water use, energy and noise, along with a half-mile setback from homes. The vote followed a public hearing on the proposed ordinance earlier in the day.

Arizona Daily Star, KGUN 9, KVOA News 4

Council takes up Safe City Action Plan. The City Council discussed a "Safe City Action Plan" that would organize existing city efforts on homelessness, drug use and crime into a single coordinated public safety strategy, according to KOLD News 13. Sun Tran's August newsletter also lists upcoming Safe City public meetings.

KOLD News 13, Sun Tran

City seeks public input on First Avenue road project. The City of Tucson is asking residents to weigh in on a major road project planned for First Avenue, KVOA reported. Details on the project's limits and schedule were not specified in the report.

KVOA News 4

U of A responds to federal Education Department request. The University of Arizona was among universities that received a communication from the U.S. Secretary of Education, including a questionnaire on how institutions support national priorities, President Suresh Garimella told the campus community. AZPM characterized the request as a "call to action" from the Trump administration.

AZPM, University of Arizona News

Deportation proceedings loom for some Tucson residents brought here as children. AZPM reports that Tucson residents who came to the country as children are facing deportation proceedings, amid shifting federal handling of DACA recipients. The report is the only local account of the cases so far.

AZPM

🚨 Public Safety

Report ranks Tucson roads second-deadliest in U.S.; police dispute findings. A new national report places Tucson's roads among the deadliest of major U.S. cities, ranking the city second, according to Tucson Spotlight. Tucson police dispute the ranking, citing their own crash and fatality data.

Tucson Spotlight

Storms triggered a string of flash flood and severe thunderstorm warnings Wednesday night. The National Weather Service issued repeated flash flood warnings for central, east-central and southeastern portions of Pima County and Southeastern Arizona Wednesday evening into early Thursday, along with severe thunderstorm warnings for damaging wind and hail. All of the warnings have since expired.

Arizona Daily Star, Arizona Daily Star

Tucson police seek new leads in 1982 cold case. Detectives are asking for information in the cold case homicide of 19-year-old Jeanne Overstreet, who disappeared in 1982, KVOA reported.

KVOA News 4

🏗️ Development & Business

Salad and Go closes all locations, including seven in Tucson; Dutch Bros to buy sites. The Gilbert-founded chain filed for bankruptcy and closed all of its restaurants, among them seven in the Tucson area. Court filings reviewed by KOLD and KVOA indicate a company tied to Dutch Bros Coffee agreed to purchase Salad and Go's Arizona and Nevada locations.

Arizona Daily Star, AZPM, KOLD News 13, KVOA News 4

Vail residents push back on golf course closure. Homeowners in the Rancho del Lago neighborhood say they learned the golf course their homes sit on will close within days, KVOA reported. Residents raised concerns about property values and maintenance of the land.

KVOA News 4

Sun on Demand adding east-side service zone. The City of Tucson announced that beginning Sunday, Aug. 16, the free on-demand transit service will launch its largest zone yet on the east side, covering roughly Tanque Verde Road to Golf Links Road and Wilmot Road to Houghton Road.

City of Tucson, Sun Tran

🎉 Community & Events

Kitt Peak telescope helps capture rare early moments of a supernova. Astronomers observed the earliest stages of a supernova explosion for only the second time ever, with observations aided by Kitt Peak National Observatory southwest of Tucson, the Arizona Daily Star reported.

Arizona Daily Star

📢 What Your Officials Are Saying

Rep. Adelita Grijalva (D-CD7). Grijalva's office said in a release that she appeared at a press conference with DACA recipients highlighting what her office described as threats facing Dreamers.

Office of Rep. Adelita Grijalva

Gov. Katie Hobbs. Hobbs announced in a release that the state, through a partnership with the nonprofit Undue Medical Debt, has erased more than $1 billion in medical debt for Arizonans.

Office of the Governor

Sen. Mark Kelly (D-Ariz.). Kelly said in a release that he and colleagues are pressing the EPA to abandon an effort they contend would weaken health protections for millions of Americans.

Office of Sen. Mark Kelly

Sen. Ruben Gallego (D-Ariz.). Gallego announced legislation his office said would increase take-home overtime pay for workers.

Office of Sen. Ruben Gallego

JoAnna Mendoza (D), CD6 candidate. Mendoza's campaign issued a statement criticizing Rep. Juan Ciscomani for promoting manufacturing gains, asserting more than 700 recent job losses in the region; Ciscomani's campaign posted nothing in the window, and neither the Hobbs nor Biggs campaigns posted in the governor's race.

JoAnna Mendoza campaign
