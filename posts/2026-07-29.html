⛈️ Weather — Tucson

⚠️ Extreme Heat Warning in effect through Sunday, August 2 at 8:00 PM MST. NWS Tucson warns of dangerously hot conditions across the Tucson metro area, Western Pima County, Tohono O'odham Nation, and surrounding regions. Temperatures of 107–112°F are expected; heat-related illness risk is rated Major to Extreme.

Today will be mostly sunny with a high near 106°F; heat index values could reach 107°F. Tonight stays warm — mostly cloudy with a low around 79°F. Thursday brings sunny skies and a high near 109°F, with Friday and Saturday climbing to 111° and 112°F respectively. No monsoon relief is in the near-term forecast; residents are urged to limit outdoor activity during peak afternoon hours and check on vulnerable neighbors.

🏛️ Government

Pima County Board of Supervisors appoints new treasurer amid legal claim from predecessor. The Board of Supervisors on Tuesday unanimously appointed Jorge Soto as Pima County Treasurer, effective immediately, filling the seat vacated when Brian Johnson resigned June 15 amid a sexual harassment investigation. Separately, Johnson has filed a Notice of Claim against the county, alleging that administrators "exceeded their authority and used unlawful, coercive means" to force him from office — a potential precursor to a lawsuit.

KGUN 9, KOLD News 13, Green Valley News

Tucson Mayor and Council approve $200M fiber-optic internet agreement. The council approved a landmark deal to bring 100% fiber-optic broadband to Tucson neighborhoods, backed by more than $200 million in private investment over the next five years, according to KVOA.

KVOA News 4

Arizona judge strikes anti-teacher-union ballot measure as unconstitutional. A Maricopa County judge ruled Tuesday that a Republican-backed ballot referral targeting the Arizona Education Association is unconstitutional because it would also effectively outlaw police and fire unions — violating the state constitution's Separate Amendment Rule, which bars measures that address multiple distinct subjects. The measure is off the November ballot.

Arizona Mirror, KOLD News 13

Sun Tran drivers ratify three-year contract, averting strike. Tucson Sun Tran bus drivers voted to approve a new three-year collective bargaining agreement that includes significant safety improvements, ending months of negotiations and eliminating the threat of a work stoppage, according to Tucson Spotlight.

Tucson Spotlight

Marana Town Council gets new member after contested July 21 election. Jackie McGuire was elected to a Marana Town Council seat on July 21, unseating incumbent Jackie Craig, who ran as a write-in candidate. Three additional council seats and the mayoral race remain to be decided, according to Tucson Local Media.

🚨 Public Safety

Downtown Tucson mass shooting raises concerns about youth violence. A mass shooting in downtown Tucson has prompted community concern about a rise in violent incidents involving teens and young adults, according to KOLD News 13. Further details on casualties or arrests were not immediately available in the report.

KOLD News 13

Tucson mother arrested by immigration agents outside Pima County courthouse. Federal immigration agents arrested a long-time Tucson resident and mother of three U.S.-citizen children outside a Pima County courthouse Tuesday, according to her daughter. A separate Arizona Daily Star report notes that a pregnant Colombian ICE detainee was released after three weeks in custody following allegations that she was violently assaulted by a Border Patrol agent during her arrest near Douglas.

Arizona Daily Star, Arizona Daily Star

Green Valley garage-sale shooting suspect arrested in Florida. A man wanted in connection with a May 15 shooting at a Green Valley garage sale that injured two people has been arrested in Florida, the Pima County Sheriff's Department confirmed. Two men have also been separately arrested in connection with the July 13 carjacking of a 14-year-old ATV rider east of Sahuarita.

Green Valley News, Green Valley News

🎓 Education

TUSD board appoints interim principal at Pueblo High School amid investigation. The Tucson Unified School District board unanimously appointed Alma Mejia-Garcia as interim principal of Pueblo High School while an investigation into the former principal is ongoing, according to KGUN 9.

KGUN 9

TUSD launches community liaison pilot to combat chronic absenteeism. The Tucson Unified School District is rolling out a pilot program that deploys community liaisons to identify and address the root causes of chronic absenteeism among students, KOLD News 13 reports.

KOLD News 13

New special-education school opens in Oro Valley. A new school focused exclusively on students with special needs has opened in Oro Valley, with administrators emphasizing an inclusive model for all students regardless of disability, according to KGUN 9.

KGUN 9

🏗️ Development & Business

Two projects reshaping Marshall Gulch and lower Sabino Canyon Road. One project has been completed and a second is underway to improve parking, driving, and pedestrian access at Marshall Gulch and along lower Sabino Canyon Park Road below Summerhaven, according to Tucson Local Media / Explorer News.

Tucson Local Media

Southern Arizona heat strains power grid; TEP has expanded storage capacity. Extreme temperatures are driving up demand on the regional power grid. Tucson Electric Power has expanded its battery storage capacity tenfold since setting a peak demand record last summer, KOLD News 13 reports.

KOLD News 13

Cushing Street Skate Park on track for December opening. Tucson's new Cushing Street Skate Park, which will feature shaded areas, is heading toward a December opening. Longtime local skaters say they hope the facility can revitalize a skate scene that has declined in recent years, according to Tucson Spotlight.
