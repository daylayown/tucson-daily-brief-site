# Pima County Board of Supervisors — What to Watch
## Tuesday, August 11, 2026 at 2:00 PM

Board of Supervisors' Hearing Room

**7 substantive items** on the agenda (7 for discussion, 0 on consent calendar)

---

## Pima County Supervisors to Scrutinize Staffing and Vacancy Policies as County Budget Pressures Mount

Pima County supervisors will dig into how the county fills — and chooses not to fill — its open positions Tuesday, a discussion with direct implications for service delivery across every county department; the board will also hear updates on homelessness, wildfire preparedness, climate action, and the county's current financial health.

---

### **How Pima County Decides When to Hire — and When to Leave Jobs Empty**
**Item 3 | Discussion**

This item asks county administrators to walk the board through exactly how staffing requests are evaluated during the annual budget process and how vacant positions are tracked and managed throughout the year. District 1 submitted detailed questions for this discussion — suggesting at least one supervisor believes the current process lacks transparency or consistency. For residents, this matters because unfilled positions translate directly into slower permitting, reduced public health capacity, stretched public safety staffing, and longer wait times for county services.

---

### **One Year of "One Pima": How Is the County's Homelessness and Public Safety Initiative Performing?**
**Item 5 | Discussion**

Supervisors will receive a full fiscal year summary of One Pima, the county's coordinated strategy linking homeless services with public safety outcomes. This is a high-profile, politically charged effort that touches law enforcement, behavioral health, housing, and community programs simultaneously. Residents who have watched encampments, emergency calls, and shelter capacity become persistent flashpoints across Tucson will want to know whether the numbers show meaningful progress.

---

### **Wildfire Risk in Pima County: A One-Year Check on the Mitigation Plan**
**Item 7 | Discussion**

One year after the county adopted its Wildfire Mitigation Plan, staff will present an update on what has actually been implemented and where gaps remain. With Tucson's surrounding mountain communities — the Catalinas, Rincons, and Tucson Mountains — increasingly exposed to fire risk driven by drought and invasive grasses, this review carries real stakes for residents in the urban-wildland interface. The board will hear whether the county is on track or falling behind on the protective measures it promised.

---

### **Pima County's Climate Action Plan: Where Does It Stand?**
**Item 6 | Discussion**

Staff will update the board on progress under the county's Climate Action Plan, which covers county operations, emissions reduction targets, and community resilience measures. As extreme heat and drought continue to define life in southern Arizona, this update offers a measure of accountability against prior commitments — and signals whether the county intends to accelerate, maintain, or quietly scale back its climate goals.

---

### **Monthly Snapshot: Is Pima County's Budget Holding Together?**
**Item 8 | Discussion**

The board's regular monthly financial update will give supervisors and the public a current read on whether county revenues and expenditures are tracking with projections. Given the budget pressures that have shadowed Pima County in recent cycles — including debates over staffing levels addressed elsewhere on this very agenda — this presentation can surface early warning signs of shortfalls or unexpected windfalls before they become a crisis.
